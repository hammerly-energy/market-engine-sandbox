Market engine

A nodal DC market clearing engine. LP dispatch, duals as prices. Five synthetic generators on a five-bus teaching case, cost-based offers. No unit commitment, no storage, no reserves.

Network

Buses and branches as the editor declares them. Colour is identity, not price — it matches the figures in src/viz/. The ringed bus is the slack, which is an accounting origin: no LMP, dispatch, flow or settlement figure depends on which bus it is.

Levers

Four of these change the scenario and re-solve on every step of the slider; the hour does not, because the engine returns the whole day at once. Nothing is debounced — at a degenerate breakpoint the price genuinely flickers between equally valid values, and that is shown rather than smoothed away.

LMP at each bus for the hour shown. Every number is a field of the clear() return, rounded — nothing on this page is computed in JavaScript.

Islands and the settlement residual

One energy balance, one λ and one settlement identity per island, per hour. λ is the LMP at that island's slack. The residual is congestion rent measured from the money — payments − revenue — minus the same rent derived from the duals, −Σ μ·f. Two independent routes to one number; it is displayed rather than hidden because a visible ≈ 0 is the claim the rest of this rests on.

Traffic

Every request carries a monotonic id and only the freshest response is applied — a drag fires many solves and they return out of order. A burst is what the solver sees during a drag: one applied, the rest dropped.
