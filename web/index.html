Market Engine

Nodal DC clearing. LP dispatch, duals as prices. Five synthetic generators on a five-bus case. No unit commitment, no storage, no reserves, no losses, no validation against real prices.

Network

A disc is a bus and its ring is the LMP; the outer ring is the slack. A square is a generator — details on hover. A wire's width is its flow and its colour the direction. A dashed ring is a price the ramp cannot carry — none yet, or one the optimum does not pin. Hover says which.

Locational Marginal Price (LMP)

Waiting for the first solve…

The dashed line is λ, the same at every bus. The bar is congestion. The DC approximation is lossless, so there is no third term.

Congestion is zero at the slack, so its price is λ. Move the slack: the bars change, the prices do not. A hollow end cap and “one of several” mean the optimum pins no single price, so the number is one end of an interval.

Merit Order

Offers cheapest first, each block as wide as its capacity and as tall as its cost. Solid ran. Dashed is demand; λ is the rule across it.

A cheap block left empty beside a full dearer one is congestion. Bids sit at the ceiling, not to scale: the offer cap is far above every offer. The cap bounds an offer. With a line binding, a bus clears wherever λ plus its congestion lands — above the cap, or below zero.

Line Flows Against Limits

Bars are the flow, on one MW scale shared by every line. Ticks are that line's rating each way. Positive runs from the branch's first bus to its second.

μ is the dual on the limit, and it is signed. |μ| is what one more MW of rating is worth; the sign says which limit is holding. No ticks means no rating, so it can never bind.

Generation by Unit

Bars are dispatch, on one MW scale shared by every unit. Ticks are capacity, so the gap is headroom. Colour is the unit's identity, as in the merit order.

Status is where a unit sits, not what sets the price: off, interior part-loaded, at_max full. rc is the LMP at its bus minus its offer.

Islands and the Settlement Residual

One λ and one settlement identity per island, per hour. The residual is rent from the money minus rent from the duals — shown, not hidden. The last column says whether the optimum pins one price and one dispatch; an island with nothing in it pins neither.
