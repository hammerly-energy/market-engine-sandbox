Market Engine

A nodal DC market clearing engine. LP dispatch, duals as prices. Five synthetic generators on a five-bus teaching case, cost-based offers. No unit commitment, no storage, no reserves, and no comparison against published prices.

Network

A bus is a disc with its name inside it; the ring around it is its LMP. The outer ring marks the slack, which is an accounting origin — no LMP, dispatch, flow or settlement figure depends on which bus it is, only the level of λ. A square is a generator; its bus, capacity and offer are on hover and on focus, because five more names would collide at this scale.

Levers

A filled thumb re-solves. The hollow one indexes a day already on the page. Nothing is debounced — at a degenerate breakpoint the price genuinely flickers between equally valid values, and that is shown rather than smoothed away.

Locational Marginal Price (LMP)

Waiting for the first solve…

The dashed line is λ, the energy price, and it is the same at every bus. The bar is congestion: what moves a bus away from it.

Congestion is zero at the slack bus, so its price is λ. Move the slack and every bar changes length while no price moves.

Merit Order

Offers cheapest first, each block as wide as its capacity and as tall as its cost. The solid part ran. The dashed staircase is demand, and λ is the rule across it.

A cheap block left empty beside a dearer one that is full is congestion: the line that would carry those MW is at its limit, so the stack is not dispatched in its own order. That is also why the intersection here is not the price at any particular bus — with a line binding there is no single marginal unit, and the LMP panel above is where the prices are. Firm bids are valued at the offer cap, far above every offer, so they are drawn at the ceiling rather than to scale.

Islands and the Settlement Residual

One energy balance, one λ and one settlement identity per island, per hour. λ is the LMP at that island's slack. The residual is congestion rent measured from the money — payments − revenue — minus the same rent derived from the duals, −Σ μ·f. Two independent routes to one number; it is displayed rather than hidden because a visible ≈ 0 is the claim the rest of this rests on.
