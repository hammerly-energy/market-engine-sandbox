Market Engine

This is a basic interactive electricity market sandbox built by Christian Hammerly. Add generators, loads, and connections to see how the local prices of electricity fluctuate. No unit commitment, no storage, no reserves, and no losses.

Price Formation Overview

LMPi=λ+CCi+MLCi
LMPi
Price to serve one additional MWh of load at node i.
i
Index representing a specific node or bus on the grid.
k
Index representing an individual transmission line in the system.

System Energy Price (λ)

The marginal cost of energy at the slack bus, ignoring grid limits. This is the same at each bus.

Congestion Component (CCi)

The marginal cost of congestion at bus i, summed over every line the network has:

CCi= ∑ k=1K (PTDFi,k×μk )
PTDFi,k
Fraction of node i's power physically flowing over line k.
μk
System cost savings if line k's capacity increases by 1 MW.

Marginal Loss Component (MLCi)

The cost of energy lost as heat in delivering power to bus i. This engine assumes negligible losses, so this term equals zero at each bus:

MLCi=λ×∂Ploss∂Pi=0
Ploss
Total physical power losses across the entire modeled grid.
Pi
Amount of real power injected directly at node i.
∂Ploss∂Pi
Change in total system losses per 1 MW injection at node i.
